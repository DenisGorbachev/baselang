# How to predict the future?

This question implies:

1. The existence of "future".
2. The existence of "past".
3. The existence of connection between "past" and "future".

If there were no "past", it would be unnecessary to predict the "future".

If there were no connection, it would be impossible to predict.

The prediction, at high level, is this:

1. Observe the previous states (the past).
2. Build a model of reality (the connection).
3. Calculate the next states (the future).

But the devil is in the details.

How exactly do we observe the past?
